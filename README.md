## 📌 Executive Summary
This repository contains the analysis and commercial insights derived from the Quantium virtual internship. The project is divided into two primary tasks: analyzing customer purchasing behaviors and evaluating the performance of trial store layouts. 

### Key Findings
* [cite_start]**Core Drivers:** Older Families (Budget & Mainstream tiers) are the primary volume drivers, generating the highest total revenue[cite: 6].
* [cite_start]**Highest Value:** Young Families spend the most per customer annually ($34.69) across all segments, indicating strong loyalty and larger basket sizes[cite: 7].
* [cite_start]**Product Preferences:** The 175g pack size is overwhelmingly the category anchor, and Kettle is the dominant brand across all analyzed customer segments[cite: 8].
* [cite_start]**Trial Store Success:** Trial stores (77, 86, and 88) showed positive directional uplift, consistently outperforming matched control stores in Total Sales, Customer Count, and Transaction Volume during the trial period[cite: 11, 68].

## 🏬 Task 2: Trial Store Performance

### Methodology
[cite_start]The trial ran from February to April 2019[cite: 64]. [cite_start]Trial stores were matched to control stores based on pre-trial sales trends, customer counts, and transaction volumes[cite: 64]. 
* [cite_start]Trial Store 77 matched with Control Store 233 [cite: 65]
* [cite_start]Trial Store 86 matched with Control Store 155 [cite: 66]
* [cite_start]Trial Store 88 matched with Control Store 237 [cite: 67]

### Performance Results
[cite_start]The trial was directionally successful, with all trial stores experiencing positive growth[cite: 90]. 
* [cite_start]**Store 77 (Top Performer):** Demonstrated a +29.13% increase in total sales [cite: 12] [cite_start]and achieved a +23.48% uplift in unique customers compared to its control store[cite: 92].
* [cite_start]**Store 86:** Achieved a +13.54% increase in unique customer visits[cite: 93].
* [cite_start]**Store 88:** Achieved a +5.75% increase in unique customer visits[cite: 93].

## 💡 Recommendations & Next Steps
* [cite_start]**Rollout Strategy:** Proceed with a cautious, monitored rollout of the new store layouts[cite: 94]. 
* [cite_start]**Statistical Caveat:** Because of the short three-month trial window, the strong percentage uplifts for Store 77 did not achieve formal statistical significance (p > 0.05)[cite: 13, 94]. 
* [cite_start]**Monitoring:** It is highly recommended to extend the monitoring period before executing a full network-wide rollout to ensure long-term sustained growth[cite: 95].
